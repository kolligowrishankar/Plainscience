# STYLE SHEET - Plain Science Shorts (APPROVED by owner 2026-11-12: always follow this style sheet)
Defaults used for sample 1 (owner did not answer section 10): ranked 3-fact format with countdown badge, yellow emphasis for facts and red for danger words, light royalty-free music.

Derived from 3 reference Shorts in `References/` (ref1 candy facts 45s, ref2 human-body facts 43s, ref3 life-saving facts 37s).
Measured: duration, resolution, scene cuts, loudness, Whisper transcripts. Judged by eye: contact sheets and full frames.
NOT measurable by me: the actual sound effects and music tracks. Audio rules below are conservative and need the owner's ear.

## 1. What the references have in common
- Fast "list of facts" Shorts: "N facts about X", each fact is one beat with its own visual.
- 37-45 s long, 125-129 spoken words = about 170-185 words per minute. Dense, no dead air.
- Hook is the first sentence and states the promise ("Three interesting facts about...", "Here are the most unusual facts...").
- The host talks in every reference. Plain Science is FACELESS, so the host cutaways are replaced by B-roll (section 6).
- Picture changes constantly: 17-28 detected cuts in refs 2-3, plus zooms and overlays inside shots in ref 1.
- Last line is a short call to follow ("Follow for more"). Plain Science rule: NO ad language, so end on the fact (see 7).

## 2. Format
- 1080x1920, 9:16, 30 fps, H.264, high bitrate (references are ~576-608 px wide; ours must be full HD, never 720p, never choppy).
- Target 35-45 s, 120-130 words at ~165 wpm, one fact per 6-8 s.

## 3. Captions (the signature element)
- Word-by-word / 2-3 words at a time, timed to real word timestamps (Whisper or Speko audio_transcribe). Never evenly split.
- Position: lower third, centred, about 70-80% down the frame (above the Shorts UI bar).
- Font: heavy rounded sans (Montserrat ExtraBold / Black or similar), UPPERCASE in refs 2 (ref 1 uses mixed case, ref 3 uses burned-in subtitles).
  Plain Science default: Montserrat ExtraBold, UPPERCASE, about 64-72 px at 1080 wide, max 2 lines.
- Colour: white fill, thick black outline (about 6-8 px) plus soft drop shadow so it reads on any footage.
- Emphasis: the key word of each phrase turns YELLOW (#FFD400) or RED (#FF3B30) (ref 2 uses yellow for facts, red for danger words).
  Max 1 emphasised word per caption.
- Motion: quick pop-in (scale 0.85 to 1.0 in about 3 frames). No long fades.

## 4. Structure and on-screen graphics
- Opening 2-3 s: the hook line, with the strongest visual and a title card or countdown badge visible.
- Countdown badge (ref 1): pink (#E86FD8) hand-drawn circle top-left with the fact number, counting 3, 2, 1. Use it when the Short is a ranked list.
- Pointers (ref 2): red arrows and red circles drawn on the footage to show exactly where to look. Use on every diagram or microscope/scan shot.
- Small credit text at the bottom of real footage when the source requires it.
- Labels (ref 2): short red label next to the subject ("SKIN MITE"). Keep labels 1-2 words.

## 5. Pace and camera
- Cut or move every 1.5-3 s. A held shot longer than 3 s must have a punch-zoom, pan or element entering.
- Punch-in zoom (about 110-120%) at the start of each fact; fast whip/blur transition (about 4-6 frames) between facts (ref 1).
- First beat hits hardest (stronger zoom). Last beat flows back to the first so it loops.

## 6. Visuals (faceless replacement for the host)
- Real footage or images of the actual subject for every beat: macro/microscope clips, medical scans, product shots, nature, 3D body diagrams.
- Sources: royalty-free or properly licensed only (Pexels, Pixabay, NASA, Wikimedia Commons with attribution, our own screen recordings and animations).
- Do NOT use film/TV clips (ref 3 relies on them; they are copyrighted and risk strikes).
- Do NOT copy the reference creators' footage, scripts, wording or branding. Only the editing style is borrowed.
- Where B-roll is unavailable, build a clean animated diagram in HyperFrames in the same colour palette.

## 7. Voice, text and ending
- Voice: warm, quick, varied pace, energetic hook; short sentences; everyday comparison before any term (existing yt-render rules).
- Hook formats that worked in the refs: "N things you didn't know about X", "Here are the most unusual facts about X", "If X happens, do Y".
- Ending: close the loop with the last fact, no "follow/subscribe" line (channel rule: no ad language). Optional: last word cuts straight to first frame.

## 8. Sound (needs owner's ear)
- Measured loudness range of the refs: LRA 1.1-3.2 LU = heavily compressed, voice-led mix; peaks -3 to +3 dBFS.
- Voice always on top. Music very low and soft under the voice (existing channel rule), ducked 20-24 dB.
- Sound effects: a short "whoosh" on each cut, a pop/click when a caption emphasises a word, a soft hit on the number badge. Subtle, never louder than the voice.
- Owner to confirm: which of the three has the sound you like best, and whether you want trending music or only light background tracks.

## 9. Quality gate (every video)
- Frame snapshots checked before the full render; captions inside safe area; no static shot over 3 s; facts verified; no copyrighted clips; 1080x1920 30 fps H.264.
- Hold for owner approval before any upload.
